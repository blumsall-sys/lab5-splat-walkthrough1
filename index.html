Settings

Preview a bundled .sog or Gaussian .ply in this browser. This local preview does not upload your file anywhere. To publish it, place the file inside the repository's assets folder and update assets/scene-config.json.

A depth proxy is required for accurate depth of field. After loading an unrelated splat, disable DoF until you supply its matching proxy mesh.

Annotations are editable in-browser. Use “Place annotation” to position a hotspot straight ahead at the nearest collision hit. Download JSON after editing and copy it to assets/annotations.json before publishing.

Desktop

Click the 3D scene to lock the mouse. WASD walks, mouse looks, Shift sprints, Space jumps, and Esc unlocks the pointer. Click a hotspot to read it.

Mobile

Use the two on-screen touch zones: left side to move, right side to look. Tap a hotspot to open its description. Mobile input comes from PlayCanvas's first-person controller.

Authoring

Export your SuperSplat to SOG. For accurate collision and autofocus, create matching proxy / collider GLBs in Blender with the included addon.
