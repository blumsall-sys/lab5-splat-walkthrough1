Settings

Preview a bundled .sog or Gaussian .ply in this browser. This local preview does not upload your file anywhere. To publish it, place the file inside the repository's assets folder and update assets/scene-config.json.

On supported GPUs, native Gaussian-splat scene depth includes buildings, trees and the ground. Other GPUs fall back to the hidden GLB depth proxy. Toggle Show scene depth to inspect exactly what the focus effect sees. Local splat previews disable DoF until the collision/depth matches the new scene.

Annotations are invisible 3D sphere triggers, not clickable UI. Approach the marker to trigger a world-space popup, or choose Interact to require E/F or a gamepad/touch action. Use Place annotation to anchor a trigger to the nearest collider, then export JSON to assets/annotations.json.

Desktop

Click the 3D scene to lock the mouse. WASD moves, mouse looks, Shift sprints, Space jumps. Walk inside an annotation's invisible sphere. Automatic annotations open on approach; manual ones show an E/F interact prompt. E/F can also close an annotation without unlocking the mouse. Esc unlocks the pointer.

Mobile

Use the two on-screen touch zones: left to move, right to look. Enter a trigger sphere and tap the Interact button to open/close the popup. Gamepad X (button 2) also interacts. You no longer need to click an annotation hotspot.

Authoring

Export SuperSplat to SOG. The splat itself supplies full-scene DoF depth on supported devices. Make a separate static collision GLB for walking, and optionally a depth-only proxy for device fallback. The Blender add-on exports sphere trigger radii and activation modes.
