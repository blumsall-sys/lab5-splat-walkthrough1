Rendering & performance

Waiting for renderer…

Native frustum culling and device-dependent splat budgets run in PlayCanvas. LOD streaming and regional culling require a compatible multi-LOD SOG/octetree asset; a single flat SOG does not contain independent chunks to stream.

The depth-of-field shader uses the scene-depth texture where supported. Focus sampling uses a separate GPU splat picker, and can differ at mixed transparent geometry. Manual and Tap Lock modes prevent changing focus during movement. On unsupported devices a hidden GLB proxy supplies depth; local splat previews still disable DoF until a matching scene is loaded.

Annotations are invisible 3D sphere triggers, not clickable UI. Approach the marker to trigger a world-space popup, or choose Interact to require E/F or a gamepad/touch action. Use Place annotation to anchor a trigger to the nearest collider, then export JSON to assets/annotations.json.

Desktop

Click the scene for pointer lock. WASD walks, mouse looks, Shift sprints, Space jumps. E/F interacts within a 3D trigger. Press R to measure and lock focus at the reticle; or select Auto, Tap Lock, or Manual in Settings. Esc releases pointer lock.

Mobile

Visible left MOVE and right LOOK joysticks follow your touches. Buttons: Jump, Sprint, Interact near a 3D marker, and Focus to lock onto the reticle. Focus scene in Settings also allows selecting an arbitrary splat surface.

Authoring

Export SuperSplat to SOG. The splat itself supplies full-scene DoF depth on supported devices. Make a separate static collision GLB for walking, and optionally a depth-only proxy for device fallback. The Blender add-on exports sphere trigger radii and activation modes.
